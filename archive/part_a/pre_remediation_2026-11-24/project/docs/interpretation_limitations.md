# Interpretation boundaries and outstanding identification problems

Stock-market prices aggregate expectations, discount rates, liquidity and news. Announcement CARs measure conditional valuation movements, not realised operating profitability, labour productivity, policy compliance costs or equity gains. Causal conclusions require stronger identification than a single-date market-model comparison provides.

Official publication dates are verified in research/events.csv but actual first-public-disclosure times are not. The 2017 charter may be anticipated after earlier approval news. The 2018 release follows earlier drafts and Cabinet approval associated with 19 September. That date is evaluated as an alternative information event without pretending the first disclosure timestamp is settled. The 2025 EE announcement overlaps a period of major international tariff/trade developments; the pipeline does not isolate those shocks. The 2026 B-BBEE bundle is broad, may contain distinct notices and has no established unaffected comparison group. Prior/next-session sensitivities do not resolve anticipation or concurrent-news bias.

IRESS Close adjustments for dividends, splits, consolidations, rights and other actions are unverified. J203's price/total-return variant is also unverified. Large raw-price discontinuities may be genuine reactions, corporate actions or errors. The baseline retains extreme moves, separately auditing all >30% adjacent returns; severe deletion sensitivity cannot establish which are economic errors. Provider definitions and influential observations require manual/source review before thesis-final results.

Industry snapshots are historical nature-of-business descriptions, not verified ICB assignments or legal Mining Charter applicability. High label concordance is provisional identity evidence. Reporting months precede announcements, but publication availability is unknown and a strict verified-availability sample is empty. Broad metal producers may differ from directly regulated miners; the narrower proxy is only a documented sensitivity. Non-mining controls may face indirect effects and industry shocks. No EE workforce target-gap or dated B-BBEE compliance exposure is fabricated.

Instrument identifiers are not permanent companies. Multiple instruments can overweight an issuer. Contemporary ISIN/listing status cannot establish historical identity or eligibility. Listing/delisting/suspension dates and ordinary/preference security status are incomplete; matched historical product flags remove some likely non-company securities without certifying all remaining ones. Historical price availability and window completeness can cause selection. The supplied observed calendar is not independently certified against the exchange calendar, and the universe may have survivorship bias.

The market index contains affected firms and can absorb broad market-wide policy reactions. An insignificant abnormal return is not evidence of no policy impact. Mining-minus-control is relative, not a total market wealth effect. Equal security weights describe the sampled securities, not economy-wide or issuer-weighted effects. Generalising from liquid listed securities to unlisted firms, workers or the South African economy is unsupported.

Bootstrap intervals assume a stable pre-event residual process, complete-case common-date representativeness and conditional market regressors. They retain common shocks and fit uncertainty but do not guarantee coverage under event-induced volatility or altered correlations. Positive-volume sample screens and null raw-return inclusion differ; assess this with the supplied sensitivity. Numerous windows/specifications are exploratory. Only five designated primary tests receive the headline Holm adjustment.

Before sending findings as thesis-final, review exact release timing, provider adjustment/return definitions, influential corporate actions, historical issuer/security/exposure matches and inference calibration with the supervisor. Dated capitalisation, leverage, book-to-market, workforce targets and B-BBEE compliance would enable advanced models; their absence does not invalidate a transparently qualified baseline descriptive event study.

A concrete classification limitation appears in the executed source: KIO:XJSE:EQU has historical label `Iron & Steel`, outside the supplied mining-candidate enumeration. It therefore enters the provisional non-mining portfolio in the baseline. The broader mineral-value-chain sensitivity moves historical Iron & Steel labels into a broader exposure proxy; it does not verify legal treatment. Review these controls before interpreting the spread as regulated mines versus unaffected firms.
