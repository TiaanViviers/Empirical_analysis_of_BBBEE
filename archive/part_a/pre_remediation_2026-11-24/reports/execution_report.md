## 6. Limitations and blockers

Existing-result limitations: raw Close/J203 adjustment conventions, unverified first disclosure times, anticipation and concurrent news, provisional industry exposure, incomplete historical issuer/security identity, post-event observability selection and conditional stationary-residual inference. Inactive-day raw residuals enter the null while positive-volume screens enter fitting/event returns; no-volume sensitivity is supplied. Event-induced volatility is not guaranteed covered.
KIO has the historical label Iron & Steel and enters the supplied non-mining proxy, illustrating potential control contamination. The broader mineral-value-chain sensitivity is not verification of legal Mining Charter treatment. Unknown securities remain unknown. Current ISIN/source-name overlap checks found no repeated groups in eligible samples, but do not certify unique historical issuers.
Advanced analyses blocked by missing data: historical market capitalisation, leverage/book-to-market, workforce/EE target gaps, B-BBEE compliance levels, legal treatment and permanent issuer histories. No fabricated substitutions were used. Strict publication-availability-verified classification analysis is unsupported because the verified subset is empty.
Environment limitation: fresh network dependency restore remains untested. Optional improvements include independent exchange-calendar/universe reconciliation, deeper heteroskedastic null calibration and corporate-action verification of influential observations. The single unavailable placebo does not invalidate the complete baseline events.
