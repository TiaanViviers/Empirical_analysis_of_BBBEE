# Validation report

**Independent review update:** The retrospective tables were subsequently compared cell by cell using a separate MuPDF extraction, and their rendered layouts inspected. The three 2022 figure tables were independently transcribed from rendered pages. See `bbbee_independent_review.md` and `bbbee_independent_cells.csv` for coverage, methods and final analytical rules. The qualifications and original checks described below remain relevant.

Reproduction checks passed for 35 retrospective tables: 11 ownership, 22 national/sector compliance count and percentage tables, and 2 sample tables. Bounding-box coordinate comparison checked 2,899 nonblank published cells, including all status counts and percentages, against the layout extraction. This second method uses the same local PDF and Poppler, so it is not an independent audit of the publisher's content or of the PDF itself. Supplementary Figures 7, 8 and 10 were visually rechecked in rendered pages: 138 values, including printed zeros; Figure 8 male plus female equals total to printed precision. All 35 retrospective tables were rendered and visually inspected for titles, labels, years and ordering; issue cells in Tables 4–5, 60 and 78–79 were checked explicitly. See the detailed verification log and `bbbee_coordinate_checks.csv`.

The pipeline checks unique keys, 0–100 percentage bounds, nonnegative integer counts, table row counts and year alignment, published count totals, source hashes, and female-vs-overall pairs. Table 1 and Table 2 published category counts sum to annual totals when numeric entries are added; dashes are retained as missing. For compliance, all 117 sector/national distributions reconcile when numeric entries are added except National 2018 and Financial Services 2016. Five Financial Services 2016 published percentages differ from count / printed total by more than 0.51 percentage points; all other eligible cells agree within that rounding tolerance, except National 2018 Level 5. The two affected distributions have no calculated percentages or Levels 1–4 summary.

Ten female retrospective averages exceed their matched overall averages: Generic 2016 economic interest; Tourism 2020 and 2021 economic interest; Agri-BEE 2019 and 2020 economic interest; Financial Services 2015 voting and economic interest, 2016 voting, 2020 economic interest; Construction 2017 economic interest. They are flagged, not corrected; a different valid indicator sample could produce this, but the report does not establish the reason. No supplementary sector female value exceeds its paired overall value.

Numerical reconciliation is necessary but cannot prove that a published number, table header, or underlying sample is correct. The same-library coordinate check and targeted rendering leave a separate human source audit useful and requested. This dataset is prepared for that independent review, not a substitute for it.
