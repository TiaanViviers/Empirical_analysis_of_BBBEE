# CEE independent review

> Final status: the ten reporting years have been checked using independent PDF text extraction with rendered-page sampling. The final assessment and filters below supersede earlier provisional assessments retained as a review history.

## Method and coverage

This is a partial review, not the requested complete cell-by-cell verification. The 19th report PDF page 106 was rendered and the two retrospective 2016 national management rows were checked across all ten demographic counts and percentages and both totals. The 24th report PDF page 28 was rendered and the 2023 coverage table's 18 sector labels and two count columns, and its total, were checked. The 2021 Table 9 (22nd report PDF page 33), 2022 Table 6 (23rd report PDF page 35), and 2025 Table 5 (26th report PDF page 36) were checked using independently read PDF layout text for the ten wrapped labels and their associated report and employee counts. Those three tables were not checked cell by cell or rendered for this review. The previous 2024 coverage visual check is recorded in the visual log, not repeated here.

The independent calculation script `analysis/part_b/tests/independent_check_cee.py` compares every derived row against the demographic CSV, checks uniqueness and ten demographic components, recomputes both representation measures and reconciles count denominators. This establishes internal consistency, not accuracy of source transcription or column assignment. It checks matching source ID and page, not full traceability of every field. The generated `cee_visual_verification_log.csv` records page-level scope; other years and management, EAP and coverage tables have not received complete independent source verification. Do not interpret numerical reconciliation as visual verification.

## Findings and corrections

- **RESOLVED — 2016 national counts omitted.** The 19th report Appendix E, PDF page 106, identifies the workforce profile for 2016, with top and senior manager totals of 60,037 and 143,706, including separately tabulated foreign nationals. All 20 counts and percentages were read from the rendered page; both counts sum to the printed totals. The extraction script now transcribes those counts and uses them for the two national indicators. The earlier published percentage sums were 28.1% and 8.5% for top, 40.0% and 14.5% for senior. Counts are now preferred; slight differences reflect rounded components. The sector observations for 2016 remain percentage sums. Reporting employers change over time; the table does not by itself establish citizenship for the demographic categories.
- **RESOLVED — ten missing coverage labels.** Wrapped names and associated counts in 2021, 2022 and 2025 were read from the cited source tables and entered into the generator using year, reports and employees as a joint key. The 2022 line-break hyphen in “Remedi-ation” was removed. Other potentially partial labels have not all been verified. These ten text checks do not establish full table accuracy.
- **SOURCE LIMITATION — 2023 coverage.** The rendered 24th report PDF page 28, Table 6, shows sector employees summing to 7,382,513 while its published total is 7,382,213; Table 5 also prints 7,382,213. The individual sector values and reported totals remain as printed, with the discrepancy flagged. Its cause cannot be determined from this table; no value was normalised.
- **BLOCKING ISSUE — source verification.** Independent review remains incomplete for the 2016 sector tables, 2017–2025 management tables, most coverage tables, and all EAP cells. Exact source pages are listed in `cee_visual_verification_log.csv` and the data source fields. Complete checking of the cells, headings, zeros, source references and labels is required before final accuracy sign-off. This is remaining work, not a claim that human intervention is inherently required.
- **BLOCKING ISSUE — 2019 management discrepancy.** The 20th report Appendix A PDF page 50 and Appendix C sector pages beginning at 62 give totals that differ by 31 top and 256 senior managers after extraction. Full independent cell and label verification is still needed before assigning the cause to extraction or the source. Do not adjust either series to force agreement.
- **SOURCE LIMITATION — historical crosswalk.** The 11 broad-sector and 18 detailed-sector systems do not have a verified employer-level transition or complete definition-based bridge. A common name alone is insufficient evidence of identical composition. No historical broad panel is recommended; no invented mapping or counts are supplied.
- **SOURCE LIMITATION — 2025 reporting population.** The previous investigation found no compatible occupational demographic counts for a stable employer subset. Retain 2025 as a separate reporting regime; this review did not exhaust all possible sources.
- **NON-BLOCKING LIMITATION — release dates.** Most exact publication dates remain unverified; publication year and workforce reporting year are distinct.

## Final descriptive readiness and exact selection rules

These recommendations concern descriptive observations from reporting employers; they are not a balanced employer panel, an economy-wide census, evidence of causality, or a legally exact citizenship-based measure. Specify the management level and indicator explicitly. Source checks use independent PDF text extraction and visual sampling, not exhaustive manual inspection of images. Treat all remaining source inconsistencies as flagged limitations.

| Series | Selection in the derived management data, unless otherwise stated | Descriptive assessment |
|---|---|---|
| National management | `geographic_scope == "National" & reporting_year >= 2016 & reporting_year <= 2024 & calculation_method == "count_based" & occupational_level %in% c("Top management", "Senior management")` | Suitable, with changing reporting populations. 2016 is retrospective from the 19th report and its workforce grand total differs from the original coverage total by 34,213; flag it and consider showing it separately. The source-defined African, Coloured and Indian categories form the black numerator; foreign nationals are included in the total denominator. |
| Detailed sector management | `geographic_scope == "Sector" & reporting_year >= 2019 & reporting_year <= 2024 & calculation_method == "count_based" & occupational_level %in% c("Top management", "Senior management")` | Suitable for descriptive observations with sector-label and definition caveats. Treat 2019 sector sums as 31 top and 256 senior managers below the national source, without forcing agreement. Do not join on row position or assume similarly named categories establish identical definitions across years. |
| 2016–2018 broad sector observations | `geographic_scope == "Sector" & reporting_year >= 2016 & reporting_year <= 2018`, preserving `sector_original` and `calculation_method` | Suitable as separately labelled source observations; 2016 uses sums of rounded percentages, while 2017–2018 use counts.
