# B-BBEE collection handover

**Independent review complete:** the retrospective series is suitable for qualified descriptive research under the exact filters and limitations in `bbbee_independent_review.md`. Exclude the unauthenticated `status_2022` series from primary analysis.

The six final CSV files are in `data/part_b/processed/`: `bbbee_ownership.csv`, `bbbee_compliance_levels.csv`, `bbbee_compliance_summary.csv`, `bbbee_reporting_coverage.csv`, `bbbee_sector_reference.csv`, and `bbbee_validation_results.csv`. Definitions, keys, missing values and flags are in `bbbee_data_dictionary.md`; coverage in `bbbee_coverage_report.md`; source limits and unresolved discrepancies in `bbbee_source_comparability.md` and `bbbee_issue_register.md`. `bbbee_verification_log.csv` and `bbbee_coordinate_checks.csv` record the current proofing. The independent review additionally used MuPDF and rendered pages; the original automated coordinate cross-check shares Poppler and the same PDF with the initial extraction.

For candidate descriptive analysis, filter ownership to `series_id == "retro_2026"`, years 2016–2023, and select each measure explicitly. A 2013–2023 extension is available with substantial regulatory and selection caveats. Inspect flagged female-overall cases and small context samples rather than automatically dropping or treating them as impossibilities; a threshold of 30 is only a screening convention. Sector series are feasible where coverage is sufficient but small and changing samples limit interpretation. Compare distributions with their contextual coverage; omit rows flagged `unreliable_distribution_count_sum` for computed National 2018 and Financial Services 2016 shares. In the summary use nonmissing `analytical_value` and inspect all flags, particularly inferred-zero and small denominators. Keep the supplementary `status_2022` ownership series entirely separate and exclude analytical missing values, including the not-analysed Generic years.

Values are repeated samples of certificate/report entries, not a balanced firm panel or South Africa's total business wealth. Total sample counts do not establish ownership-indicator n. Do not invent weights, standard errors, confidence intervals, or a CEE-sector crosswalk. The 2022 source's local authenticity remains unresolved, as described in the issue register.

Reproduce from the repository root with `sh scripts/part_b/bbbee/reproduce_bbbee.sh`. Python 3 and Poppler `pdftotext` are required; no additional Python packages. A dedicated virtual environment can be created with `python3 -m venv .venv-bbbee` and activated before running the script. CSV output is used because a Parquet engine is not installed. The pipeline verifies both PDF hashes, regenerates pilot and final datasets, checks row/column positions, and writes the verification log. Originals remain local and unchanged; check redistribution rights before sharing them. No network access or TLS bypass occurs in reproduction.
