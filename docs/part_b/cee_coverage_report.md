# Coverage — work in progress

All ten reports have been acquired. National and sector top and senior management observations and national EAP values have been extracted for 2016–2025. Sector coverage is 11 per year in 2016–2018 and 18 per year in 2019–2025. The 2016 national observations are retrospective and 2016 sector observations are percentage-only; 2018 national observations are count-based but its 11 sector observations are percentage-only because of PDF text encoding. The other years have count-based management records. Reporting coverage is much less complete: national 2016–2025 and 2024 sector coverage only. Full visual validation and sector comparability are unfinished, so no historical series is yet recommended for analysis.
