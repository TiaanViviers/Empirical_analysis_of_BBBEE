## Employment Equity source checks

- Draft table renders were rotated 90° clockwise. Physical PDF pages 4, 6, …, 38 map to printed gazette folios 22, 24, …, 56. For each sector the visual review confirmed the sector heading, Top and Senior Management blocks, National rows above the provincial section, workforce rows below it, BLACK column to the right of W, and Male/Female/Total order. Cell OCR on a separate crop pass checked all 216 cells. Higher-resolution renders resolved OCR errors in decimal punctuation, percent glyphs and one dropped decimal separator. The verification input records those decisions.
- All eight supplied Agriculture draft and baseline anchors match. The proposed and observed BLACK totals equal male plus female in all 72 applicable draft/profile groups at published precision. A component rounding example occurs in Manufacturing Top Management National Male. Displayed A, C and I give 24.9% when added, while BLACK is published as 25.0%. The extract keeps 25.0%.
- Final physical PDF pages 4–8 map to printed gazette folios 6–10. The rendered pages verified the numbered sector headings, designated-group columns and the first two sets of Male, Female and Total rows. All eight supplied total anchors match. All 36 totals equal the corresponding male and female sum at published precision.
- The sector reference covers 18 unique source labels in each series. Trade is supported by all four within-report comparisons of the full Top and Senior Management percentage vectors. All 36 sector and occupational-level groups in each of the two CEE reporting years have complete, reconciling count inputs for the proposed race-by-sex proxy.

### Historical attachment limitation

The Q3 2025 release title was visible in the Stats SA search index. Direct access returned a challenge page and the page’s actual attachment href could not be confirmed. Direct attempts at the spaced and unspaced XLSX filenames also returned HTML. The 2024 official page exposed its XLSX link, and that direct download also returned challenge HTML. The collection uses the complete five-PDF fallback. It cannot establish any workbook precision or historical revision differences.

To obtain the preferred workbook for a future vintage comparison, open the Q3 2025 release in a regular browser, select **QLFS Trends 2008-2025 Q3** under **Additional download(s)** and save the unmodified file together with the final download URL. If the 2025 workbook remains unavailable, use the 2024 listing and its confirmed XLSX link. The fallback has no missing observation years.
