## Validation and caveats

Check all extracted cells independently against rendered pages, with a separate verification log and source-table keys; validate chronological column order, original precision, totals, derived percentages, null conventions, key uniqueness and ownership female/overall comparisons. Investigate discrepancies rather than silently changing values. In particular, 2018 Level 5 = 14 but 6% is published, and status counts sum 2,473 against 2,605. Four Financial Services female averages exceed overall; unmatched valid denominators may explain these or there may be error. 2013 and pre/post-2015 comparability is poor. There is no microdata or ownership-specific sample count identified, and the averaging weights are not documented. Small sector-year counts, changing code thresholds and scorecards, and selection mean neither a balanced panel nor national wealth totals are supported. Do not create confidence intervals from the aggregate tables.

Original files are ignored by Git because public access does not waive copyright restrictions. Verify reuse rights before any redistribution. Most Commission-hosted files were downloaded with TLS verification bypassed due to certificate-chain failure; their hashes establish local integrity but do not authenticate origin. Readiness is conditional on independent pilot proofreading and retaining the documented limitations; source acquisition itself is complete except optional missing 2020 and later reports.
