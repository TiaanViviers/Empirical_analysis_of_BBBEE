# B-BBEE extraction progress — preliminary, not review-ready

Reproduce from the repository root with `sh scripts/part_b/bbbee/reproduce_preliminary.sh`. Requires Python 3 and Poppler `pdftotext`. Original publications are not changed. Existing pilot outputs are regenerated. This is not the requested final pipeline; the `_preliminary` datasets have not received the required independent extraction comparison or complete validation.

`bbbee_ownership_preliminary.csv` contains 468 retrospective cells from Tables 16, 23, 28, 35, 42, 50, 60, 67, 74, 81 and 88, plus 138 pilot chart observations. The script checks years, four columns, and unique keys; Property 2013 has a blank fourth cell, checked using PDF text coordinates. All table pages have been read as layout text, but rendered, cell-by-cell verification remains outstanding. The chart transcriptions are reused without a new visual recheck. Ten female averages exceed the matching overall average: Generic 2016 economic interest; Tourism 2020 and 2021 economic interest; Agri-BEE 2019 and 2020 economic interest; Financial Services 2015 voting and economic interest, 2016 voting, 2020 economic interest; Construction 2017 economic interest. Indicator-specific denominators and weights are unavailable, so the causes remain unresolved.

`bbbee_sector_compliance_preliminary.csv` holds 1,060 cells including totals from Tables 20–21, 26–27, 32–33, 39–40, 47–48, 57–58, 64–65, 71–72, 78–79 and 85–86. Year headers and row lengths are checked, but no complete visual proofreading has been performed. Count dashes remain missing, not assumed zero. For a diagnostic sum only, numeric cells are summed without dashes. Financial Services 2016 numeric counts sum to 9 while Table 78 publishes total 10; Table 79 percentages correspond approximately to denominator 9. This does not establish a correction. Five percentage discrepancies use the printed total 10 as denominator; the entire distribution is flagged. Do not use calculated percentages or derived summaries for that distribution.

The pilot national compliance flags 2018: counts sum to 2,473 versus 2,605, while Level 5 is 14 and 6%. No correction established; the distribution should be excluded from derived shares. Further investigation against rendered original and related tables is still required. The national pilot did not automatically flag the entire 2018 distribution, so do not treat its individual flags as an adequate filter.

The original acquisition register reports a certificate-verified download of the retrospective. A new attempt to retrieve both files with ordinary curl failed at DNS resolution. The official department PDF was accessible via the web reader and its title/content match, but the reader does not supply bytes for hash comparison. The Commission-hosted 2022 report timed out in the web reader and its local file's authenticity remains unverified. SHA-256 values in the source inventory identify local files, not independent authentication. No TLS bypass is used in these scripts.

Outstanding: Table 1 extraction; final standardized national compliance, coverage, summary, sector reference and validation datasets; robust treatment of all missing and dash cells; rendered comparison of every requested table and supplementary figure; investigation of 2018 and ownership anomalies; complete verification log, data dictionary, methodology, validation, coverage, issue register and handover; final reproducible pipeline and final dataset review. Do not use preliminary datasets for analysis until these steps are completed. Existing comparability documentation explains source limitations and separation of the series.
