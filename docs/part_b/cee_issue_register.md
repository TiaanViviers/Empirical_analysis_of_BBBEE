# CEE issue register — final collection review

- **RESOLVED — source checking:** all 2016–2025 extracted management cells, sector coverage labels and counts, national coverage counts, and EAP values and reference periods were independently checked using PDF text extraction; representative pages were rendered. The review is not exhaustive visual proofreading. See `cee_independent_review.md` for the year-by-year method and counts.
- **RESOLVED — wrapped coverage labels:** ten missing 2021, 2022 and 2025 labels and seven truncated 2020 labels were reconstructed from their original source rows and are reproducible; original fragments are retained where extracted.
- **RESOLVED — 2016 national count omission:** the 19th report retrospective 2016 counts were transcribed from rendered PDF page 106 and are used to calculate national indicators.
- **SOURCE LIMITATION — 2016 source difference:** original coverage is 7,071,449 while the 19th report retrospective workforce grand total is 7,105,662; the 34,213 difference is unexplained and flagged. The cited 2016 sector tables provide percentages only.
- **SOURCE LIMITATION — 2019 management:** published sector totals are 31 top and 256 senior managers below the published national totals. The independently checked source tables contain these numbers; the cause is unknown. Do not force reconciliation.
- **SOURCE LIMITATION — 2023 coverage:** published sector employee counts sum to 7,382,513, 300 above the published 7,382,213 total. Retain both.
- **SOURCE LIMITATION — 2024 percentages:** 37 demographic published percentages differ from count-derived values by more than 0.051 percentage points. Use valid count-based derived indicators and preserve published values.
- **SOURCE LIMITATION — 2025 reporting regime:** designated-employer criteria changed, with exceptions for organs of state and employers designated by collective agreement; an identified compatible historical employer subset is unavailable. Treat 2025 separately.
- **NON-BLOCKING LIMITATION — sector definitions:** no historical broad-to-detailed sector mapping is established; harmonisation was outside scope. Similar labels do not prove stable sector membership.
- **NON-BLOCKING LIMITATION — publication dates:** exact dates remain unverified for most reports. Publication year and workforce reporting year are separately recorded.
