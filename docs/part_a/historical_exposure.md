# Historical securities, issuers and policy exposure

The baseline remains the event-specific **source industry proxy**, with equal
security weights. Its mining/non-mining categories are not verified uniform
legal treatment versus unaffected firms. Unknown historical identities remain
unknown. No current listing status or current-name guess determines eligibility.

`research/security_exposure_review_v1.csv` is an event-dated, versioned ledger.
It records identity, class, verified issuer links, industry/geographic evidence,
legal-exposure status, confidence/review status, source URL, review date and
unresolved questions. Review evidence can be retrospective; verified publication
availability before each announcement is a separate issue and remains missing.
The source classifications and `research/events.csv` are not rewritten.

The `reviewed_industry_proxy` sensitivity moves KIO into the mining proxy using
the issuer's [2017–2021 Sishen social and labour plan](https://www.angloamericankumba.com/~/media/Files/A/Anglo-American-Group-v9/Kumba/sustainability/sishen-mine-social-and-labour-plan-2017-2021.pdf).
It excludes Hulamin from the direct-mining proxy, leaving it unknown rather than
asserting it is unaffected, using its [2017 manufacturing operations report](https://www.hulamin.com/iar2017/strategy/chief-executive-officers-report.php).
These are industry-based alternatives, not a certification of identical
incremental Mining Charter obligations. The broad mineral-value-chain and
strict-mining sensitivities remain separately labelled.

The AMS→VAL [JSE notice](https://clientportal.jse.co.za/Content/JSENoticesandCircularsItems/JSE%20Clear%20Market%20Notice%2016225%20EDM%20-%20Treatment%20of%20Anglo%20American%20Platinum%20Ltd%20%28AMS%29%20-%20Corporate%20Action%20-%20Name%20Change%20-%2028%20May%202025%20%28Ex-Date%29.pdf)
supports identity continuity at the 2025 change. It does not alone validate every
earlier vendor splice or the event-dated bulletin join. VAL, ANG, BHP and NPH
therefore retain unresolved historical mapping questions. Current mining-related
names are not sufficient to reassign them automatically.

The [Fortress JSE notice](https://clientportal.jse.co.za/Content/JSENoticesandCircularsItems/JSE%20Market%20Notice%2049117%20EDM%20-%20Treatment%20of%20Fortress%20Inc%20Fund%20Ltd%20A%20%28FFA%29%20and%20Fortress%20Inc%20Fund%20Ltd%20B%20%28FFB%29%20Corporate%20Action.pdf)
establishes the A/B issuer relationship. `partial_verified_issuer` gives each
verified issuer cluster one equal portfolio unit, split equally among its
complete classes. Every unmapped security remains a separate unit. This is
**partial issuer-aware weighting**, not certification of the complete universe
of issuers. A spread normalises each side separately.

MTNZF's BEE-vehicle and unwind history is explicitly recorded. It remains in the
baseline security population; exclusion of unresolved action securities is a
sensitivity, not a claim that investment-company ordinary shares are invalid.
The relevant [issuer capital-distribution notice](https://senspdf.jse.co.za/documents/SENS_20250715_S507582.pdf)
also warns that the scheme's valuation basis changes during its unwind.

Outputs include the executed security review, primary security/industry/group
contributions, alternative industry definitions and partial issuer weights.
Historical ordinary/preference classes, all issuer links, legal/geographic
intensity and EE/B-BBEE exposure remain incomplete. No workforce, compliance or
financial-control values have been invented.
