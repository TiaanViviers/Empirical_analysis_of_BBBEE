Daily equity returns are calculated from historical closing prices obtained through IRESS Expert. The adjustment conventions of the exported Close field have not been independently verified. Consequently, returns are interpreted as price-based returns rather than dividend-reinvested total returns. Potential corporate-action distortions and non-trading observations are identified through data-quality screening, with affected observations investigated and sensitivity analyses performed. The FTSE/JSE All Share Price Index (J203) serves as the market benchmark.
