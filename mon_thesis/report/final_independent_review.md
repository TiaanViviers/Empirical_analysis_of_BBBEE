# Final independent academic and methodological review

**24 November 2026 · Stellenbosch University**  
*Empirical Analysis of the Efficiency–Equity Tradeoff Associated with B-BBEE Policies in South Africa*

## Submission decision

**Ready for submission as the combined descriptive research report.** The report answers how selected JSE securities moved around five publication dates and how participation in management and ownership changed in the populations covered by the published data. Its market estimates are conditional on the event design. Its equity series describe reported outcomes. Identifying a causal efficiency–equity tradeoff would require realised efficiency measures, credible policy exposure and a counterfactual.

No critical or major issue remains. The review found no substantive error in the approved empirical results. It corrected the report’s regulatory description and publication chronology, clarified weighting, uncertainty, figure selection, placebo accounting and sourcing, and rerendered both formats. ## Methodological judgment and remaining limits

- The event study is suitable for describing market-model relative movements among observable securities. Liquidity and price availability shape the samples. Historical classifications approximate mining exposure and non-mining securities can be indirectly affected. Earlier disclosures and concurrent shocks constrain attribution. Incomplete corporate-action information limits interpretation as investor wealth changes. Pre-event bootstrap dependence may fail to describe event-period volatility, as the calibration illustrates.
- The CEE denominator includes all reported posts at each level. Reporting employers and employee composition change, particularly in 2025. The ownership data come from verification submissions with unknown indicator weights and denominators. The reconciled contributor-level distributions measure a broader scorecard outcome. These limits matter for generalising beyond the published populations.
- The 2023 target gaps compare published sector aggregates. The 2025 management proxy lacks statutory citizenship and joint disability information, and the 2024 workforce profile became public after the April announcement. Employer-level applicability and compliance require different information. The QLFS is a household survey with distinct industry definitions and publication vintages. These are limits on interpretation that the final report discloses.
